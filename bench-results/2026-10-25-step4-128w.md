# 128-writer embedded write benchmark (Step 4)

Apple M2 (8 cores), 8 GB RAM, macOS Darwin 25.1.0 arm64, rustc 1.92.0, release
profile. One stream, unique keys, 200 single-record appends per writer, 128
writers, fresh temp dir, background compaction disabled, `max_index_ram_mb=1024`.

| 2 | 12,829 | 9,748.48 | 14,753.79 | 100 | 128.00 |
| 3 | 14,480 | 8,847.36 | 12,681.22 | 113 | 128.00 |
| 4 | 7,338 | 13,803.52 | 54,034.43 | 57 | 128.00 |
| 5 | 8,655 | 14,008.32 | 29,540.35 | 68 | 128.00 |

Median **12,093 ops/s**; range **7,338–14,480** (≈ ±30%). p99 range
**12.68–54.03 ms**. The spread tracks the host's fsync rate (57–113/s), so these
numbers are disk-bound and should not be compared across machines.
